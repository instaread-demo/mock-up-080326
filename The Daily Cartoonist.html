Webcomic Cartoonists on Parade at the Webcomicon

Skip to comments
D. D. Degg Published: July 27, 2026 1 minute 0 comments

Webcomicon comes to Ferndale the weekend of August 1st and 2nd!

A host of some of the most famous webcomic creators will gather for the 2026 Webcomicon:

Creators include Sarah Anderson, Asher Perlman, Dave McElfatrick, Jim Benton, Nick Seluk, Harris Fishman, Dee Parson, Shen, Nicholas Gurewitch, Kim Winder, Thorn Knight, Julia Kaye, Brian Gordon, Jejo Sleeper, Justin Westover, Zach Stafford, Katie Cook, Jeff Lilly, Aaron Reynolds, Jimmy Craig, Chris Grady .Ryan Rds, Hollering Elk, Ryan Keats, Ellis Rosen, Neil Kohney, Dana Atnip, Tom Savage, Bret Juliano, Scott Hall, Mark Dudley, and maybe more.

There are scheduled events.

Get your tickets here.

Included with Your Ticket:

  • Show floor access at the Rust Belt Market. Meet creators, get signed merch, prints and originals
  • Attend insightful and inspiring panels about the comic industry, personal experiences, publishing and more at 215 West.
  • Participate in all-ages drawing workshops by top industry professionals at Painting with a Twist
  • Watch hilarious cartoonists battle it out in LIVE DRAW events where the audience gives suggestions (18+ Live Draw Battle Saturday, All ages Sunday)
  • Win valuable art and memorabilia
