HCPO: Harrison man arrested on child sex-abuse charges already out of jail

27 July 2026
By Kevin A. Canessa Jr. | Editor & Broadcaster

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:45
Instaread

A 49-year-old Harrison man has been arrested on sexual abuse of a minor charges, Hudson County Prosecutor Wayne Mello said.

On Thursday, July 23, 2026, members of the Hudson County Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force arrested Daniel Depasque, of Harrison, and charged him with one count of endangering the welfare of a child/possession of child sexual abuse material

He was arrested at his residence and transported to the Hudson County Correctional Facility, pending his first court appearance.

He has since been released from custody, according to jail records provided by Vinelink.

According to Mello:

In May 2025, members of the Hudson County Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force received information child sexual abuse material was identified as being possessed at an address in Harrison. During the course of the investigation, more than 1,500 items of child sexual abuse material were located belonging to Depasque.
